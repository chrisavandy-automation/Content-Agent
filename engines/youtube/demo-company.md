## The business
- **What:** air source heat pump surveys, installs and yearly servicing.
- **Where:** West Yorkshire. Towns for the search pages: Leeds, Bradford, Wakefield, Huddersfield, Halifax, Harrogate, Dewsbury, Keighley, Pontefract, Otley.
- **Two kinds of customer:**
  1. **Homeowners** (most of the work): owner-occupied semis and terraces, 1930s–1970s, replacing an old gas boiler.
  2. **Landlords and letting agents** (the B2B side): portfolio landlords and property managers looking after several rentals.
- **The offer on every page and ad:** a free home survey. One call to action: book the survey.
- **Price and grants:** [install price range] · [current Boiler Upgrade Scheme grant: check gov.uk on the day, don't quote from memory].
- **Proof:** none real. Any review, number or case study in the demo is invented and must be labelled as invented.

## Who buys and why (homeowners)
- Their gas boiler is old or has just failed.
- Their worries: "will it heat a draughty old house?", "is it noisy?", "will my bills go up?", "is the grant real?", "will the installer disappear after it's in?"
- What they want: a warm house, lower running costs, someone local who explains it straight.

## Who buys and why (landlords and letting agents)
- They manage several properties and have to plan heating upgrades, energy ratings and boiler replacements.
- What they want: one installer for a batch of properties, booked around tenants, with the paperwork done.
- **Buying signals for system 3:** a letting agency or property manager hiring a property manager or maintenance coordinator in West Yorkshire; a new block of rental flats in planning; a landlord posting about boiler or energy-rating problems.

## Competitors (for the ad teardown)
Big national energy brands that sell heat pumps, plus local installers. Use their **public** ads from the Meta Ad Library. Show their ads only to analyse them; never copy wording into Hollowmere's ads.

## Voice
Plain, local and straight-talking. No green-guilt messaging, no hype words. Says what it costs and what's awkward (the outdoor unit, the bigger radiators).
