## Platform notes (carried over from LinkedIn-Agent; update as I learn)
- The hook must fit above "see more": about 2 short lines on mobile.
- One sentence per line; line breaks between points; 0-2 hashtags.
- During Publish30, the last line is the counter: `#Publish30 N/30` (N = the day's number, not the post count).
- Posting window that's worked: 5-7pm UK; repost the next morning.
- Images: 4:5 for carousels/infographics; blurred screenshots for builds. My real photos live in LinkedIn-Agent `photos/` (reference by name, never copy).
- Results to log after 48h: reactions / comments / reposts, plus impressions and saves when I have them.
- Drafts: `engines/linkedin/drafts/`.
