## 2. The transformation (niche = the person they become, not the topic)
- **A (before):** the weekly numbers arrive late and don't reconcile, compliance issues get caught at the final review, builds wait behind copy, the Head of Growth chases everything, and repeatable work is done by hand in chats.
- **Z (after):** one report the team trusts, ads that ship clean, pages live on time, procedures a new hire can use on day two, and every repeatable job automated with AI.
- **In one line:** from a marketing machine that runs on people chasing things to one that runs on systems.

## 3. The association I want
The person who makes the marketing machine run: numbers you trust, systems that don't break, ads that ship clean. Automation first, AI as the layer.
Branding = pairing. Keep pairing my name with: working builds, real numbers, clean systems, Claude used on real marketing work.

## 5. PTL: how each piece builds influence
### Power (show I have what they want)
What they want **[CONFIRM: these are my best guess from the role]**:
- External: a report they trust, campaigns live on time, fewer hires needed, hours back each week, a career that grows.
- Internal: control, calm, being the one who's on top of it, looking good in front of the founder.
How I show it: verbally (time saved, volumes, things shipped, Publish30 milestones) and visually (the dashboard, the workflow, the screenshot).

### Trust (directions they can follow and win with)
Step-by-step directions I can give **[CONFIRM / add]**:
- Turn a prompt you paste every week into a skill, then a routine.
- Check a process by hand before you automate it.
- One naming convention for every campaign.
- Copy-paste prompts and SOPs (e.g. the board of advisors prompt, the free Claude session).

### Likeness (in-group)
- External: UK, 30, casual, talks like a mate sat next to you at the screen, self-taught on the tools.
- Internal: keep it simple, verify before automating, one clear focus, automation only amplifies what's already there.

## 6. ARR: the content types
| Type | Job | Formats |
|---|---|---|
| Authority | Show competence | Teach something tactical, personal lessons, build breakdowns, client-work lessons (anonymised) |
| Reach | Get seen | Trend-jacking (AI news), humour, shareable one-liners |
| Resonance | Build connection | Personal stories (I thought X, now Y), core values, contrarian takes |
Mix (from LinkedIn): about 1 build post per 3-4 learning or life posts, a weekly milestone, light posts at weekends.

## 7. Stance (while I'm employed at Systemise and Scale)
- Content documents what I learn and build. No pitching services, no "DM me to work together", nothing that reads as my own consultancy or agency.
- Never vent about clients, even anonymised. Never name clients. Never post SAS's internal plans.
