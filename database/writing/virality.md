# The three viral formats (used as lenses, never as scores)
Read by: the hooks skill and the write skill. Source: `sources/ai-assist-and-viral-formats-lesson.md`; the 12 moves for value hooks: `sources/viral-hook-science-deck.md`.

## My rule: I judge, the AI doesn't score
No virality scores, no "this one scores 8/10, post it". I don't know how an AI would judge them, so the decision stays mine.
What a skill does instead: label each hook with its lane, name its **weakest lever** in plain words ("the gap isn't wide enough: no before number"), and suggest the one move that would lift it. Then I choose.

## The three lanes
| Lane | Formula (the levers) | A hook in this lane... | Weakest-lever fixes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Value** | Benefit × Relevance × Credibility ÷ Perceived effort | promises something so useful they'd save it or send it to their team | Lead with the outcome, not the method · name the reader · one real number · make it look easy (fewer steps, "in 10 minutes") |
| **Emotion** (the course calls it shock) | Emotional charge × Relevance × Novelty | makes them feel something strongly: annoyed, heard, fired up, surprised | Turn up the intensity · pick a clear side · make it a purple cow (would they have seen this before?) |
| **Story** | Transformation gap × Relevance × Novelty | opens a before → after so wide they need to read on | Open on the before, at the lowest point · tangible numbers for scale · a before/after image |
Relevance is in all three: speak to my reader (`foundation/pillars.md`), use their lingo, hijack a trend where it's true.

## The hook flow (from one angle)
1. **Angle first.** One sentence: the idea, the reader's promise, and the ONE emotion it's for (OHHHH · FINALLY · WTF · YAY · WOW · LOL · AWW, from `writing/gate.md`). If any is unclear, fix the angle before any hooks.
2. **15 hooks, 3 lanes × 5:** Value 5 · Emotion 5 · Story 5.
   - Each lane = **4 proven + 1 wildcard** (`system/wildcard.md`). Proven = borrows a named example from `examples/` or one of the hook types in `writing/hooks.md`.
   - At least one `plain` hook across the set (`writing/hooks.md`, my rules).
   - Every hook passes `writing/anti-ai.md` (run `tools/slop_check.py`).
3. **Show them as three short lists**, each hook tagged: hook type · proven/wildcard (+ the example it borrows) · weakest lever in a few words. No scores.
4. **I play:** pick, mix, rewrite. Then the chosen hook's lane sets the body (`writing/structures.md`):
   - Value → listicle, icp-callout, carousel
   - Emotion → hot-take, meme, x-screenshot
   - Story → transformation, moment-led story, day-x, life-update
