# The publish gate (one checklist for everything)
Read by: the review skill, on every draft (posts, scripts, captions), before I see it as "done".
Sources: `sources/5c-writing.md` (5Cs), `sources/editing-checklist-lesson.md` (Jay's editing checklist), plus `writing/anti-ai.md`, `writing/virality.md` and `foundation/pillars.md`.
This is the ONLY pre-publish checklist. Other files point here instead of keeping their own.

## Two kinds of check
- **AI checks (pass/fail):** the reviewer marks each one PASS or FAIL with the line that fails and a fix. Any FAIL is fixed before I see the draft.
- **My calls (questions):** the reviewer asks me, and gives its honest read, but never answers for me or scores the post. I decide.

## Mindset (5Cs)
Online, nobody owes you their attention. Write for someone busy and distracted, at about a 5th-grade reading level. The goal is clarity, not art: the copy is a shop window, so the reader looks through it at the idea, not at the glass.

## A. The 5Cs (AI checks)
| C | Pass when | How to check |
|---|---|---|
| **Clear** | One idea (rule of one); passes the firefighter test ("There's a fire downstairs. Open the window and jump."); reading level around grade 5 | Remove what's unclear, editing from the end back to the front |
| **Concise** | Nothing can be cut without losing meaning | Every word costs $10: list what could go |
| **Concrete** | At least one number, name, tool or picture; no vague "grow / help / transform"; big ideas zoomed into one specific moment | Could the reader picture it? |
| **Conversational** | Sounds like me and uses my audience's words | Read it out loud; anything nobody would say gets rewritten |
| **Cadence** | Sentence lengths vary; no flat block | Read out loud (stumbles get rewritten) + the squint test on the shape |

## B. Hook, body, CTA (AI checks)
- **Hook:** callout and promise are clear in the first line (`writing/hooks.md`); its lane's weakest lever is named (`writing/virality.md`), not scored.
- **Body:** uses a skeleton: What / Why / How (teaching) or But / Therefore (story). No linear "and then… and then…".
- **CTA:** at most one: one ask, one reader. Usually a question, a save, or a follow. Never a sales pitch (`foundation/pillars.md` stance).

## C. Anti-AI and guardrails (AI checks)
- `python tools/slop_check.py <file>`: zero hits, or each hit justified.
- The pass/fail gate in `writing/anti-ai.md` (first person, no em dashes, no invented stats, something only I could write).
- Pillars stance: no SAS clients or internal plans; no named business I've looked at; nothing from a Story Bank row marked Private; side business and £10k goal stay out until I confirm.

## D. My calls (asked, never scored)
1. **Emotion:** which one is this for? OHHHH (now I get it) · FINALLY (someone said what I feel) · WTF (that annoys me) · YAY (great news) · WOW (that's amazing) · LOL (so funny) · AWW (so cute). Does the draft actually deliver it? If "not really", edit.
2. **Objections:** is any obvious "yeah, but…" left unanswered? Have I looked at it from another angle?
3. **Interesting:** would former me enjoy this? Would my idols? Would future me be proud of it?
4. **Elegant:** is there a place for a comparison, a metaphor or a bit of rhythm? Do I sound like the writer I want to become?
5. **Trust or authority:** does it help people know me (trust) or prove I can do it (authority)? Which pillar and which P/T/L?
6. **Only me:** is there something here only I could have written? Would I save, share or comment on it in my own feed?
7. **Binge test:** does it fit what my last 20 pieces say I help people do?

## Output (what the reviewer hands back)
One card: A-C as PASS/FAIL (with fixes applied or proposed), then the D questions with the reviewer's one-line read on each. No overall score, no "ready to post". Then my final read out loud.
