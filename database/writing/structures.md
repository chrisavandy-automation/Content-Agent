# Post structures (bodies)
Read by: any skill that writes a post body or plans a format. Read `foundation/pillars.md` first; the hook comes before the body (`writing/hooks.md`).
Source: `sources/post-structures-lesson.md` (Jay Yang). Not exhaustive; new formats appear every week.

| Tag | Structure | Best for (pillar · story type) | How to write it |
|---|---|---|---|
| `announcement` | Announcement | 1 build · 4 who I am · momentum: shout | First person in the hook. Under ~10 lines. A personal twist in the hook or ending. Tip: framing a post as an announcement helps it reach more people. |
| `transformation` | Transformation (before → after) | 3 finding · 4 who I am · far-past | Open on the before; hint at the after in the hook. Add a before/after image (e.g. the old process vs the new system). |
| `listicle` | Listicle | 2 how to · 3 finding | Personal ("3 things I used to…"). Lived-experience value, not cookie-cutter. 3-5 points, line breaks between lines. |
| `life-update` | Life update | 4 who I am · momentum: win, remind | Casual and short. Shows momentum without a full announcement; post often. |
| `hot-take` | Hot take | 3 finding | Pick a side, clearly, in the hook. Give the reasoning. Only takes I actually believe. |
| `carousel` | Carousel / infographic | 2 how to | Start from what the reader most wants to know; pack it in. Reuse a long piece (a video, a how-to) rather than remake. One point per slide (carousel) or dense (infographic). 4:5 or 9:16. Made by the artifact skill. |
| `day-x` | Day X of doing Y | 4 who I am · future · momentum | An ambitious goal as the Y. Document daily what moved me closer. (e.g. #Publish30) |
| `icp-callout` | ICP callout | 2 how to | "If I were [who] and wanted [outcome], here's what I'd do:" Call out the ICP in the hook; one specific problem, one tangible fix. |
| `meme` | Meme | Reach, any pillar | 1-2 lines max. A relatable moment from my audience's day. Timely. Funny, not goofy. |
| `story` | Moment-led story | 4 who I am · 3 finding · any story type | Open in the moment (scene first), But/Therefore through the middle, end on a human line or an honest question, not a neat lesson. |
| `x-screenshot` | X screenshot | Reach · AI news | Use X to validate (what worked for my ICP there). Short caption, or add a lot of value. |

## Body skeletons (inside any structure above; from the 5Cs)
- **What / Why / How** (teaching): the point → why they should care → how to do it. ("Drink more water. It helps you think clearly and boosts energy. Drink a glass before every meal.")
- **But / Therefore** (story): alternate "but" and "therefore" to build tension. Never "and then… and then…".

## Rules
- Pick the structure after the hook, from the same tags: the examples index shows which hook + structure pairs have worked.
- 70/20/10 applies here too: most posts use a structure that's proven for me; now and then try one I haven't (`system/wildcard.md`).
- Platform notes (lengths, image ratios, video versions) live in `engines/<platform>/`, not here.
