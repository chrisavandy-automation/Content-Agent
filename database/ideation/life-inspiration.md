# Ideation: inspiration from my life
Read by: the ideate skill (Sunday ideation, or "what should I post?"). Read `foundation/pillars.md` first.
Source: `sources/inspiration-from-your-life-lesson.md` (Jay Yang).

## Principle
The best content zooms in on small, ordinary moments, not big flashy ones (11 of Jay's last 20 posts were small moments). Doing cool things offline matters, but noticing what's already happening, and sharing it relentlessly, matters just as much.

## Where the raw material lives
- **Story Bank:** at the end of each day, 5 minutes: the ONE most story-worthy thing. A few words or a sentence, enough to trigger the memory. Voice is fine (WisprFlow or a voice note). Jay keeps one Notion page per day, titled by date.
- Current bank: Notion → Content Engine → **Story Bank** database (21 entries moved from the old .docx on 25 Oct). Claude or Grok fills it; see `database/README.md` for the columns.
- At ideation, read the last 7 days of entries first, then older ones that match a story type that's been missing.

## The 6 story types (tag every idea with one)
| Tag | Type | Opens like | Prompts to ask me |
|---|---|---|---|
| `far-past` | Far past | "When I was 15..." | What did I try years ago that failed but taught me a lesson I still use? · What small moment from when I started shaped how I work now? · What did I believe about success when younger that turned out wrong? |
| `recent-past` | Recent past | "Last week..." | What in the last week or two surprised me or made me rethink something? · A recent decision: what was in my head, how did I land on it? · A small win this week that taught me something? |
| `current` | Current | "I woke up this morning..." | What did my morning look like, and what does it say about the life I'm building? · What am I working on that a younger me wouldn't believe? · What am I doing today that moves me toward a bigger goal? |
| `future` | Future | "In 2027, I have a goal of..." | One goal this year and why it matters · Where in 12 months, and what's the first step? · If next year goes exactly right, what does life look like and what did I do? |
| `client` | Client / people I help | "Someone asked me..." | A question I get over and over, and my answer · Someone I helped recently: problem and fix · A common mistake in my world, and what I tell people instead |
| `hypothetical` | Hypothetical | "Imagine you asked me..." | A big goal from scratch in 90 days: what would I do? · Advice on the thing I know best: the steps · Starting over tomorrow: what would I do differently? |

Guardrails from `pillars.md` apply: `client` stories never name the business (and never SAS clients); `future` goals about the side business / £10k stay private until I confirm.

## The momentum loop (for anything I'm building or launching)
Tag: `momentum:<stage>`.
1. **Ask** (validate): would people want this, and what would they want to see?
2. **Whisper:** I'm working on something, no details.
3. **Tease:** it's this thing, but not much more.
4. **Shout:** it's live; be everywhere.
5. **Win:** share results (it working, people winning).
6. **Remind:** months later ("6 months since I started my YouTube channel").
7. **What's next:** say what I'm doing next. 🔁
At ideation, list anything currently in the loop and which stage is due next.
