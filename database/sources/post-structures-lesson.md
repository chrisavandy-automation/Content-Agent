# Post Structures That Work (LinkedIn course lesson, Jay Yang)
Fed in 2026-10-25. Raw source: don't edit. Distilled into `database/writing/structures.md`.
Caveat from the lesson: not exhaustive; new formats appear every week. Higher-level concepts behind the 30-day template list and the 100 templates in the Bonus Module.

## 1. Announcement
One of the highest-performing formats LinkedIn was built on (new job, personal update, milestone).
- First person in the hook (I, my). Keep it succinct: under ~10 lines. Keep it fun: a personal twist in the hook or ending.
- Examples: Kevin Shim job (linkedin.com/posts/kevinshim1_i-just-started-my-full-time-marketing-job-share-7360554205679423488-1qKM), Kevin moving (kevinshim1_excited-to-announce-im-finally-moving-to-activity-7454952597167210496-vDt-), founder milestone (johnghu_we-just-crossed-500m-made-for-creators-on-activity-7490421325438803968-8s7Y).
- Pro tip: anytime I want a post to perform a bit better, I frame it as an announcement; it gets categorised as one and pushed to more people.

## 2. Transformation
A clear before / after; builds authority and feels authentic.
- Start with the before ("3 years ago, I had applied to 500 internships and gotten rejected from every single one"). Hint at the after in the hook ("This year, I landed 5 job offers without applying… here's why:"). Always add a before/after photo.
- Examples: Kevin (kevinshim1_at-19-i-applied-to-40-internships-and-got-activity-7472702036942274560-VUr2), founder (myan_before-i-turned-down-a-230000-job-offer-share-7341547405638627328-gH6w).

## 3. Listicle
Educate with a numbered list.
- Make it personal ("3 tips I used to land a job"). Unique value from lived experience. Visually clean: 3-5 points, line breaks between lines.
- Examples: Kevin NYC (kevinshim1_i-just-spent-the-last-30-days-working-in-activity-7467266230693875712-KKD_), Kevin job search (kevinshim1_3-years-ago-i-was-trying-to-land-my-first-activity-7476325916340539392-wzdH).

## 4. Life Updates
Momentum without a full announcement; post these more often.
- Keep it casual ("Just crossed a personal milestone and hit 10,000 followers"). Keep it short.
- Examples: Jay (jayyanginspires_choosing-to-quit-my-job-and-move-back-home-activity-7443363712658436096-sT66), Kevin 2025 wrapped (kevinshim1_my-2025-wrapped-graduated-college-activity-7412603252799799296-QGpa).

## 5. Hot takes
One of the most viral formats.
- Take a stance: no lukewarm opinions; be clear what side you're on, especially in the hook. Give reasoning. Be honest: no rage-bait you don't believe.
- Example: Kevin NYC (kevinshim1_after-spending-the-last-30-days-in-nyc-i-ugcPost-7466999501468094464-1Sbo).

## 6. Carousels + Infographics
Often among the highest performers.
- Start from the ICP: what's most valuable to them; pack in relevant value. Adapt content: repackage a newsletter, thread or short-form. Reuse > remake. Canva; profile photo; one main point per slide (carousel) or jam-packed (infographic). 4:5 or 9:16 ratio.
- Example: Jade (jadebonacolta_how-to-accelerate-your-dream-life-ugcPost-7470813868408279040-rCjT).

## 7. Day X of doing Y
Uncommon, converts well for followers.
- Start from an ambitious goal (what you're working towards or away from) as the Y. Document, document, document: daily content from what you did to get closer.
- Example: Ray (fuchengrui_day-16-after-quitting-meta-i-did-zero-work-activity-7407148550112833536-AF9_).

## 8. ICP callouts
Call out the ICP, solve their problem, show why you're the best person. "If I were X and wanted Y, here's what I'd do:"
- Call out the ICP in the hook; solve a very specific problem with a tangible solution.
- Example: Eric (ericlay-virio_if-i-were-the-cmo-of-lovable-heres-how-share-7363236133125689344-bDrm).

## 9. Memes
Clear, relatable, actually funny.
- Speak to the ICP's daily experiences. 1-2 lines of text max. Be timely (a recent meme / event).
- Examples: Michael (myan_job-applications-in-2026-be-like-activity-7457576119664414720-pe5Y), Zuckerberg (myan_before-vs-after-using-simplify-to-job-search-activity-7356454438141636608-Hkxr).

## 10. X screenshots
LinkedIn trends run ~6 months behind X.
- Use X to validate: if it performed for your ICP on X, it's likely to on LinkedIn. Short caption, unless you add a lot of value.
- Example: Jia (jia-chen-in_how-do-you-get-an-internship-at-a-startup-activity-7487129692723101696-uZ8T).
