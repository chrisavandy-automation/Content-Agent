# Bonus: Editing Checklist (LinkedIn course, Jay Yang)
Fed in 2026-10-25. Raw source: don't edit. Distilled into `database/writing/gate.md`.
"This is the editing checklist I go through before I publish anything."

## ❤️ Emotions: what emotion am I trying to evoke in my reader?
1. OHHHH 😲 - now I get it
2. FINALLY 😅 - someone said what I feel
3. WTF 🤬 - that pisses me off
4. YAY 🤩 - that's great news
5. WOW 😱 - that's amazing
6. LOL 😂 - that's so funny
7. AWW 🥰 - that's sooo cute

## ❓ Questions
Is it clear?
- Am I following the rule of one?
- Are there any potential objections unanswered?
- Am I forgetting to look at my post from a different angle?
Is it interesting?
- Would former me genuinely enjoy this piece of content?
- Would my idols enjoy this?
- Would future me be proud of this piece of content?
Can I say it more concisely?
- If every word cost $10, what could I remove without losing quality?
Can I say it more elegantly?
- Alliteration. Metaphors. Rhyme, and Rhythm. Comparisons.
- Do I sound like the writer I want to become?
Does this help me build trust or authority?
- Does this help my audience get to know me better? (Trust)
- Does this help me prove my expertise and competence? (Authority)
