## The equation (slide 3)
**Virality = (Benefit × Relevance × Credibility) ÷ Perceived Effort**
Same as the Value format in M3 L6. Every hook fix pulls one of these 4 levers.

## The 12 moves (❌ weak → ✅ strong, examples paraphrased)

### 1. Increase credibility (= my "proof" ingredient)
- **1a. Use numbers.** "I help people lose weight" → "lose 10 lbs of fat in 8 weeks". "More sales" → "increase your sales conversions by 37%".
- **1b. Borrow authority.** Open with a known name's claim, then bridge to your how-to (Naval: the internet enables 1 billion one-person companies → "few people know where to start. Here's how:").
- **1c. Share personal experience.** "Everyone should have X" → "I've built 3 X, each making $15k/mo. 10 steps to start yours:".

### 2. Increase benefit (= my "promise" ingredient)
- **2a. Focus on the outcome, not the method.** "Here's my Daily Accountability System" → "How to finally feel confident in the mirror (using my Daily Accountability System)". The system goes in brackets; the outcome leads.
- **2b. Appeal to emotion.** "The basics of software development" → "build web apps from scratch and land high-paying developer jobs (even with no experience)". Name the dream, not the topic.
- **2c. Reduce uncertainty.** "Hate feeling unproductive? Here's the best way…" → "I've helped 36+ entrepreneurs overhaul their workflow. Let me show you how I do it:". A track record removes the "will this work for me?" doubt.

### 3. Increase relevance (= my "callout" ingredient)
- **3a. Speak to your avatar.** "9 of the best books on writing" → "Writers: the best way to improve your writing is to read great writing. 9 books (recommended by Hemingway):". Name the reader in the first word.
- **3b. Hijack trends.** Add the worry everyone has right now: "…(even in an AI-driven world)".
- **3c. Use their lingo.** Write how the audience talks, not how an expert explains ("Tried pre-workout ONCE and don't think I can ever go back…").

### 4. Decrease perceived effort (= my "plan" ingredient)
- **4a. Make it simple.** "43 things to keep in mind" → "a full step-by-step guide… (in under 30 days)". Fewer, clearer steps beat a long list.
- **4b. Save them time.** Swap a borrowed proverb for "I spent the last month trying to answer X. 9 techniques:". You did the work so they don't have to.
- **4c. Use social proof.** "5 tips to lose 8 lbs" → "This is Sarah. Busy mum and CFO. In 12 weeks we helped her lose 8 lbs. Here's how:". A named, relatable person proves it's doable.

## How the coach uses this
- Hook battle-test (step 4): for each hook, tag which of the 4 levers it pulls and the weakest one. The ONE fix = the move from the weakest lever.
- Mapping to my ingredients: Credibility = proof · Benefit = promise · Relevance = callout · Effort = plan.
- My guardrails still apply: numbers must be real (no invented stats, 90% lesson); social proof = anonymised clients only; no absolute SAS numbers without Gary's OK.
