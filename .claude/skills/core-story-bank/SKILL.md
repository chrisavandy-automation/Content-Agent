# Story Bank: log one moment
Reads: `database/ideation/life-inspiration.md` (the 6 story types), `database/README.md` (Story Bank columns and privacy rules).
Writes: Notion Story Bank, `collection://a4aea437-a40f-4443-990a-7dbb6790e6f7`.

1. Take what I said. If it's several moments, ask which ONE was most story-worthy (or log each only if I say so).
2. Keep my words and the real details (times, numbers, names of tools, what was said). Never tidy them into marketing language or add anything I didn't say.
3. Fill the row:
   - Moment: a few words that trigger the memory.
   - Date: the day it happened (today unless I say otherwise).
   - Story type: one of the 6. Pillar: one of the 4 (`database/foundation/pillars.md`).
   - Privacy: Private for anything about my employer's plans, clients, job hunting, or my side business and £10k goal (until I confirm otherwise); Anonymise for anyone else's business; Ask first if unsure; otherwise OK to post.
   - Story: what happened. Notes: corrections, checks, anything to verify.
4. Show me the row in 3 lines (moment · type/pillar/privacy · first line of the story), then save it.
Done when the row is saved and I've seen it.
