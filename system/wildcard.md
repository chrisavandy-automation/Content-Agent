# Wildcards
References stop the system going generic, but too many references make it copy itself. Wildcards keep fresh ideas coming.

## The mix: 70 / 20 / 10
- **70% proven**: follows a reference that has already worked (a hook formula, a post structure, a format from the database).
- **20% adjacent**: a proven shape borrowed from another niche or platform, or a twist on one of mine.
- **10% wildcard**: something I've never tried.

## How skills apply it
Whenever a skill pitches options, it labels each one PROVEN, ADJACENT or WILDCARD.
- 5 options (e.g. hooks for today's post): 4 proven + 1 wildcard.
- 10 options (e.g. content ideas): 7 proven, 2 adjacent, 1 wildcard.

## What counts as a wildcard
- It follows none of the references for this job. Name the closest one and say how it breaks from it.
- It still fits a pillar and the transformation in `database/foundation/pillars.md`. Different, not off-brand.
- It's something I could really post: true to my experience, in my voice.

## When a wildcard wins
If I pick a wildcard, or it performs, offer to add it to the references so it becomes proven next time.
